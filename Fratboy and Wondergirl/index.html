Fratboy and Wondergirl

A Daily Nebraskan satire of Greek life at UNL. Fratboy just wants a fun, lighthearted night out with Wondergirl. But on Greek Row, there is no such thing as a “normal” night out. The invite went out on GroupMe and the cover was paid on Venmo, but the bad decisions are still made the old-fashioned way. And these days, it feels like a UNL frat can barely make it through a semester without getting kicked off campus.

Escape all 23 houses, dodge the DD and Nationals and find out whether the night can actually stay fun. Fratboy can handle red water, Wondergirl can handle blue and nobody survives green.

Know before you go

  • Red diamonds are Fratboy’s. Each one is a free beer.
  • Blue diamonds are Wondergirl’s. Each one is a free shooter.
  • Red water is safe for Fratboy. If Wondergirl falls in, the night ends. Diagnosis: gonorrhea.
  • Blue water is safe for Wondergirl. If Fratboy falls in, the night ends. Diagnosis: chlamydia.
  • Green water ends the night for both of them. Your frat gets kicked off campus or put on probation.
  • The DD shows up later on. If the Designated Driver catches you, he takes you back to where you started. Jump over him.
  • Jungle juice spills slow you down and weaken your jump.
  • Blackouts and Nationals walkthroughs also crash the party as the night goes on. The group chat warns you first.
  • Kegs can be pushed. Buttons work while someone stands on them. Levers stay where you leave them.
