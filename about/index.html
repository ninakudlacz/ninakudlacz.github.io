My story

I grew up in the Omaha area, and I started my own photography business, Nina Kudlacz Photography, in 2021. Working with clients taught me something I still rely on today: the best photos happen when people feel comfortable, so my first job is always to make the space feel easy.

At the University of Nebraska-Lincoln, I found a home at the Daily Nebraskan, first as a photographer, then as Senior Multimedia Editor. There I hire, lead and coach a team of twenty, plan our weekly coverage, and look for new ways to weave photo, video and design into reporting so a story lands harder than text alone could.

I'm studying journalism with a focus on advertising and public relations because I love the whole arc of a message: finding the story, shaping it, and getting it in front of the people who need to see it. I'm also minoring in French, because I'd like to tell stories in more than one language.
