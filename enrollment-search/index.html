How many students are in each major?

Search for a major to see its fall enrollment from 2016 to 2025.

    See the numbers by program

    Counts are students enrolled in each major in the fall term. A student with more than one major is counted in each. Majors that changed names or codes over the years may appear as more than one entry, and a gap in the chart means the major wasn’t offered or had no students that fall. Raikes and Omaha program variants are combined with their main major.
