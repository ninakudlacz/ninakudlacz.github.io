        How to play

        1. Look at the photo. Click it to see it full size.
        2. Click the map where you think it was taken. Both City and East campus count.
        3. Pick the year it was taken with the slider.
        4. You get up to 5,000 points for location and 5,000 for the year. Within about 25 meters, or the exact year, earns the full 5,000.

        The daily game is the same for everyone and can be played once a day. Your streak counts the days in a row you’ve played.
