    How “right” is scored

    Each call is checked against the average temperature for February and March in the predictor's state, compared with that state's 1991–2020 normal. A shadow (six more weeks of winter) is right if those two months ran colder than normal. No shadow (early spring) is right if they ran warmer. Years exactly at normal aren't scored. Canadian predictors aren't scored because this uses U.S. data; Washington, D.C. uses Maryland's figures. “Most accurate” ranks predictors with at least 5 scored years. Because the weather outcome is a coin flip, 50% is what guessing earns.

    Predictions: groundhog-day.com. Temperatures: NOAA NCEI Climate at a Glance. This is our own scoring, not an official one.
